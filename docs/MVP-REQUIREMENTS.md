# Medical Triage MVP Requirements

## Product scope

The system supports medical triage in Bolivia with four roles: administrator, medico, paciente, and ayudante.

## Roles and permissions

- **Administrador**: manages users, roles, account status, and account configuration. Does not access clinical records.
- **Medico**: searches and attends any patient, manages clinical information, conducts triage, reviews LLM output, and confirms final classifications and recommendations.
- **Paciente**: self-registers and views only approved personal results, summaries, classifications, recommendations, warning signs, and next steps.
- **Ayudante**: can access any patient with limited clinical permissions; can view basic data, symptoms, vital signs, triage status, approved results, and recommendations, and can enter preliminary data. Cannot change final classifications or view private physician notes or LLM analysis.

Public registration always creates a paciente account. Medico, ayudante, and additional administrador accounts are created by an administrator. One default administrator exists at initial setup.

## Identity and authentication

Registration fields:

- Full name (required)
- Date of birth (required; age is calculated)
- Sex: Masculino or Femenino (required)
- Bolivian CI, numeric only and unique (required)
- Address (optional)
- Globally unique username (required)
- Password (required; minimum 8 characters, including letters and numbers)
- Password confirmation

Usernames allow letters, numbers, `.`, `-`, and `_`; spaces and other symbols are rejected.

Patients recover passwords with username + CI. Medico and ayudante password resets are managed by an administrator. Password changes are not available inside the authenticated system. The CI cannot be edited by normal users; only an administrator can correct it.

Administrators can activate, deactivate, or block any account. Account status is checked at login and authorization time.

## Patient management

Medicos and ayudantes can search any patient by CI or partial name. Search results include enough identity information—at minimum name, CI, and date of birth—to prevent selecting the wrong patient.

Clinical history is cumulative. A new triage creates an independent record and never overwrites previous triages.

## Clinical information

The patient profile includes explicit instructions and examples for:

- **Medical history**: diseases, surgeries, hospitalizations, and relevant diagnoses.
- **Allergies**: medication, food, or substance reactions.
- **Current medications**: name, dose, and frequency.
- **Chronic conditions**: ongoing or permanent conditions.

The system provides explicit alternatives such as “No known allergies”, “No current medications”, and “No relevant history”.

## Triage workflow

1. The medico selects a patient and reviews the cumulative history.
2. The medico updates relevant antecedents for the consultation.
3. The medico starts a new triage.
4. The medico records structured essential symptoms and a free-text description.
5. The LLM analyzes the available information and generates additional questions when needed.
6. Questions are shown together in a prioritized list. Questions are immutable; the medico answers with free text and may select “No aplica”, “No se conoce”, or “No se pudo evaluar”, and add observations.
7. The LLM produces a preliminary classification: leve, moderada, grave, or crítica.
8. The medico reviews the classification and may change it. An override requires a justification.
9. The LLM generates draft recommendations.
10. The medico reviews, edits, and approves recommendations.
11. Only the approved result is published to patients and ayudantes.

The LLM is assistive and never replaces the medico. Automatic critical-case alerts are out of scope for the MVP.

## Triage states

- Borrador
- En progreso
- Pendiente de revisión
- Aprobado
- Cancelado

Approved triages are locked. Corrections create a new version and do not overwrite the approved version.

## Results and history

Only results generated by triages performed inside the system are stored. Approved results are permanent and cannot be deleted. The history records the patient, date/time, medico, antecedents, symptoms, questions and answers, model output, final classification, recommendations, and version state.

Patients see only the approved, understandable result and recommendations. Medicos see the complete clinical triage, including model analysis and private notes.
